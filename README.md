# Physical AI — An Open Course

An open, public educational resource on **Physical AI**: the intersection of artificial intelligence with robotics, sensing, actuation, and the physical world.

> 🚧 Work in progress. The course structure and content are being defined incrementally.

## Publishing policy

This is a **public knowledge site**. All content is intended for open publication.

**No confidential, proprietary, or Intel-internal material, and no secrets of any kind, may ever be committed to this repository.** Working notes that are not for publication go in the gitignored `private/` folder only. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
